Privacy Policy

Hari ng Kalsada · K&A Studios · Last updated: November 2026

Summary

Hari ng Kalsada does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

  • Your game saves, settings and progress are stored only on your own device (in the browser's local storage).
  • This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export a save code or share a link or photo yourself.
  • You can delete it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app.

Sharing features

When you tap a share button (ghost time trial link, photo, bug report), the game opens your device's own share menu or copies text to your clipboard. Where it goes is your choice; the game does not send it anywhere itself.

Spoken radio news

The optional radio news voice uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. Depending on your device, the operating system may process this text according to its own privacy policy. You can turn it off in Settings.

Hosting

The game files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. The game itself does not receive or use these logs.

Children

The game contains cartoon violence and is intended for teens and older. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Contact K&A Studios through the page where you found the game.
