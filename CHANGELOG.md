# Changelog — Hari ng Kalsada

## v1.8: Kabanata 3 — Sugbo (2026-11-25)
- 🏝 **Chapter 3: Sugbo**, a new Cebu-inspired Visayan city across a sea strait east of San Isidro: the port with containers and Tiburon's warehouse, Kolon (old downtown), Plaza Krus, IT Park towers, markets, a hospital and a police station, the Busay hills with a winding road up to the Tops lookout, and an airport with a runway. It has its own traffic (multicabs, taxis, habal-habal), pedestrians and police
- ✈️ **Travel**: fly from the Manila Airport (south of Pasay) for ₱500, or take the ferry from the new San Isidro pier for ₱150. You can also cross the strait in any boat
- 📖 **8 new missions** (22 total) against Kapitan Tiburon's smuggling syndicate: Biyahe sa Sugbo, Habal-Habal (timed motorbike rides), Mata sa Pantalan (night stealth photos), Habol sa Dagat (speedboat chase), Bagyong Agila (typhoon rescue), Pista sa Sugbo (tail an armored van through a parade), Kuta ni Tiburon (warehouse gunfight) and Hari ng Dagat (yacht boss chase)
- ⚖️ **Choices carry over**: if you sold the titles in Chapter 2, Don Severo shows up as a mini-boss with extra guards; if you helped the town, the farmers send grenades. A **final choice** (expose the ledger or take ₱100,000) leads to **4 final endings** with a summary of your choices
- 👥 New characters with portraits and Bisaya lines: **Inday Bebing**, **Dodong** and **Kapitan Tiburon**
- 🚐 New vehicles: **Multicab**, **Speedboat** and the **yacht**. A **boss health bar** for big fights
- 🗺 The big map opens on your region, with a **Whole map** toggle
- 🏆 3 new achievements (Bayani ng Sugbo, Tunay na Hari, Frequent Flyer)

## v1.7: Baril, Bagyo at Pista / Guns, Typhoons & Fiestas (2026-11-24)
- 🔫 **Guns**: Pistola, Shotgun, SMG (auto), Riple (auto) and Granada. Magazines, reserve ammo, reload (`G`), tracers, muzzle flash and shell casings. Desktop: aim with the mouse, click / `X` to fire (hold for automatics). Touch: the fire button auto-aims at the nearest threat
- 🚗 **Drive-by**: fire the Pistola or SMG from any vehicle (mouse, `X`, or the new 🔫 touch button)
- 🦺 **Bulletproof vest** (absorbs 70% of damage, blue HUD bar) and **🔫 Armas ni Kabo** gun shops (2 locations, open after Mission 2) with damage/rate/range stats
- 🚓 Police notice **gunfire within earshot**. At high stars cops fire **real bullets** instead of rubber pellets (Normal 5★, Mahirap 4★, never on Madali)
- ☠️ **3 Kuta ng Sindikato** (gang hideouts) with armed goons. Fighting them isn't a crime; clear one for ₱3,000. 🥫 **Shooting range** mini-game at the gun shop
- 🚑 **5 new vehicles**: Ambulansya, Fire Truck, Trak ng Basura, Padyak (pedicab) and Pickup. Emergency vehicles use sirens in traffic; press `H` to toggle your own siren. Depots respawn them
- 💼 **New side jobs**: 🚑 Paramedic (patients to the hospital), 🚒 Bumbero (park near the fire and the hose sprays automatically), 🗑 Basurero (garbage route to the dump)
- 🌀 **Typhoons**: a PAGASA-style warning, then a storm with wind that pushes cars and people, flying yero and branches, heavy rain, lightning and **citywide flooding** (12 extra streets). 🛟 **Rescue** job: carry stranded people to the ⛺ Evacuation Center in a jeepney, van, bus or bangka
- 🎉 **Pista sa Tondo** parade every third day at 4–7 PM (floats, a marching band, dancers and crowds; walk with the parade for tips). 🎄 **Parols** on lamp posts during the "ber" months; 🕯 **Undas** candles around the church (Oct 28 – Nov 2)
- 🎤 **Videoke** rhythm game (3 original songs with lyrics, scored 0–100 like a real machine) and 🏀 **3-Point Shootout** at basketball courts
- 👗 **Wardrobe**: the Ukay-Ukay is now a shop where you choose. 8 new outfits (Filipiniana terno, Gilas jersey, camo, flannel...) and 7 accessories (salakot, shades, bandana, face mask, headphones, gold chain, bimpo). Owned items are free to re-wear, and head accessories change your **face** clue for the police
- 📷 **Photo Mode** (`O` or the pause menu): freeze time, hide the HUD, pan/zoom, 6 filters, optional frame, save as PNG
- 🏆 **15 new achievements** (38 total); a day counter; typhoon/fiesta toggles in Developer Mode

## v1.4: Bagong Home Page at Madaling Laro / New Home & Easier Play (2026-11-24)
- 🏠 **New home page**: live police-chase scene in the background, "last game" card (missions, money, % complete), big ITULOY button, quick Settings / How-to / What's-new buttons, responsive for phones
- 💪 **Choose difficulty** when starting a new game: 😎 Madali (recommended), 🙂 Normal, 🔥 Mahirap
- 🚨 **Escaping is easier**: difficulty now controls cop speed, number, sight range, how fast they lose you, search-zone size, how fast stars drain, and when helicopters/roadblocks/spikes/pellets appear. Normal is also easier than before
- 🧭 **Easy driving mode** (default): point where you want to go, on touch or keyboard; the car steers itself. If there's no road that way, it keeps straight and turns at the next intersection (yellow arrow)
- 🛟 **Driving assist**: more grip, auto-straightening on roads, automatic braking before walls. Classic controls are still available in Settings

## v1.3: Negosyo, Trabaho at Save / Business, Jobs & Saving (2026-11-24)
- 💾 **Save & load**: 3 manual slots + Auto-save (every 45 s and whenever you leave or close the app). Saves your exact spot, current car (with upgrades), active mission (resumes at its checkpoint), money, weapons, properties, garage, tsinelas, achievements and time of day
- 🔨 **Weapons** at the Hardware: pamalo (bat), tirador (slingshot, auto-aim) and water gun (soak people until they slip). `Q` / 🔄 to switch
- 🔑 **Properties**: Sari-sari Store, Carinderia, Talyer sa Paco, Beach Resort, Condo sa Makati. Daily income at 6 AM, and each works as a safehouse
- 🅿️ **Garage**: store up to 6 cars at home, the Talyer or the Condo
- 🎨 **Car customization** at Pintura: paint, engine (+8%/lvl), armor (+35%/lvl), stickers, horns. 50% off if you own the Talyer
- 💼 **Side jobs** (`J` / 💼): Food Rider, Taxi, Tow Truck, Pulis Vigilante, and **Underground Races** at night with bets
- 🏆 **20 achievements** and a **% completion** tracker
- 🚔 At 5–6 stars, police fire rubber pellets
