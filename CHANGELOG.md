# Changelog — Hari ng Kalsada

## v1.3: Negosyo, Trabaho at Save / Business, Jobs & Saving (2026-11-24)
- 💾 **Save & load**: 3 manual slots + Auto-save (every 45 s and whenever you leave or close the app). Saves your exact spot, current car (with upgrades), active mission (resumes at its checkpoint), money, weapons, properties, garage, tsinelas, achievements and time of day
- 🔨 **Weapons** at the Hardware: pamalo (bat), tirador (slingshot, auto-aim) and water gun (soak people until they slip). `Q` / 🔄 to switch
- 🔑 **Properties**: Sari-sari Store, Carinderia, Talyer sa Paco, Beach Resort, Condo sa Makati. Daily income at 6 AM, and each works as a safehouse
- 🅿️ **Garage**: store up to 6 cars at home, the Talyer or the Condo
- 🎨 **Car customization** at Pintura: paint, engine (+8%/lvl), armor (+35%/lvl), stickers, horns. 50% off if you own the Talyer
- 💼 **Side jobs** (`J` / 💼): Food Rider, Taxi, Tow Truck, Pulis Vigilante, and **Underground Races** at night with bets
- 🏆 **20 achievements** and a **% completion** tracker
- 🚔 At 5–6 stars, police fire rubber pellets

## v1.2: Buhay na Lungsod / Living City (2026-11-24)
- 🌅 **Day/night cycle**: a 12-minute day with sunrise, bright daytime, an orange dusk and lamp-lit nights; clock on the HUD
- 🌧 **Rainy weather**: rain, lightning and thunder; slippery roads; cops see you less in the rain; umbrellas on pedestrians
- 🌊 **Floods**: some streets flood in heavy rain and slow cars down
- 🚦 **Traffic lights** at 45 intersections; AI cars stop on red
- 🚗 **Rush hour**: more traffic at 7–9 AM and 5–7 PM, quieter streets after midnight
- 🥣 **Taho vendors** (morning) and 🥚 **balut vendors** (night): buy for health
- 🏀 **Basketball courts** with kids playing during the day
- 🙏 **Devotees** at Quiapo church plaza
- 👮 **Traffic enforcers** at avenue intersections (they witness crimes)
- 🏝 **Isla Maligaya**: a new island in Manila Bay with a village, resort, lighthouse and beaches
- ⛴ **Ferry** to and from the island (₱50) and drivable 🛶 **bangka** boats
- 🚤 **Police boats** chase you on the water
- 💡 4 new tips; "What's new" screen now has a tab for each version

## v1.1: Music & Quality of Life (2026-11-24)
- 🎵 **Music engine** (synthesized, no files): title theme, lo-fi walking track, and chase music that grows with your stars
- 📻 **Car radio**: HIMIG FM 98.7 (OPM pop), BUDOTS 101.1, KALYE RAP 89.5, with DJ lines and fake ads. `R` / 📻 to switch
- 🔁 **Mission checkpoints**: ULITIN restarts a failed mission at its checkpoint, skipping the dialogue
- ⚙️ **Settings**: music/SFX volume, walking music, difficulty (Madali / Normal / Mahirap), graphics quality, joystick size, left-handed controls, tips
- 💡 **Tutorial tips** shown once for new players
- 🚗 Sports car is sturdier; cops scale speed with star level
- 🐞 Fixed: failing the finale showed the ending; losing the race still unlocked the sports car
- ✨ Version number and "What's new" screen on the title menu

## v1.0: First release (2026-11-24)
- Manila-inspired open city: 16 districts, traffic, jeepneys, tricycles, pedestrians
- 6-star wanted system with suspect clues (CCTV, clothes, face, car), search zones and disguises
- Police AI, roadblocks, SWAT, helicopter, spike strips, army trucks
- 8 story missions, jeepney pasada, car selling, 20 Gintong Tsinelas
- Keyboard + touch controls, minimap, full map, auto-save
